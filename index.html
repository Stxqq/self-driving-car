How it learns

Nobody shows the network how to drive. A population of random networks takes to the road, and the ones that get furthest become the parents of the next generation.

  1. 01Sixty cars with random weights start on the same road.
  2. 02Each drives until it crashes, falls behind a line moving at 18 km/h, or a minute is up.
  3. 03Its score is the distance covered, minus 300 m for going out early.
  4. 04The best four carry over. The rest are children of tournament winners, crossed and mutated.

The trained driver

The brain in Watch came out of a longer curriculum, run headless in Node: an empty road first, then traffic, then five-minute episodes. These are its numbers on 30 roads it never saw. Traffic doesn't see it and never makes room for it.
